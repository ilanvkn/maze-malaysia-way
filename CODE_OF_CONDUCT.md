# Community conduct

Maze welcomes Malaysians and contributors everywhere, across languages, backgrounds and experience levels. Help people participate without having to prove they belong.

Discuss the work directly and respectfully. Critique code and claims with examples. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, spam and publishing private location data are not welcome. Do not pressure people to share home/work coordinates or personal travel histories.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Explain moderation decisions where it is safe and practical; apply the same standard to maintainers. Use GitHub's private reporting and blocking tools for sensitive conduct reports. Do not publish private incident details in an issue. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome.
